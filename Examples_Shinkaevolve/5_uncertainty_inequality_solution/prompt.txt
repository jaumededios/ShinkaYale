You are improving a Hermite-polynomial search for the uncertainty inequality constant C4.

The evaluator calls run_uncertainty() and expects:
- coeffs: a 1D array of coefficients for H0, H4, H8, ...
- c4_bound: the value r_max^2 / (2*pi)
- r_max: the largest positive sign-changing root of P(x) / x^2

The evaluator appends one extra Hermite coefficient to force P(0)=0, flips the
sign so the leading coefficient is positive, then recomputes r_max exactly with
SymPy. The reported c4_bound and r_max must match that recomputation.

The goal is to minimize c4_bound. The score is benchmark / c4_bound, so larger
is better and scores above 1 beat the benchmark 0.3215872333529007.

Keep the code correct, short, deterministic, and easy to understand. Favor
simple coefficient searches, structured template coefficients, small local
refinements, and robust root computations over large dependency-heavy training
loops.
