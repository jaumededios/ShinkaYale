You are improving a point-placement program for the Heilbronn triangle problem.

Place 11 points on or inside the equilateral triangle with vertices
(0, 0), (1, 0), and (0.5, sqrt(3) / 2).

The evaluator calls run_heilbronn() and checks that:
- the points stay inside the triangle
- the reported normalized minimum triangle area is correct

The goal is to maximize the normalized area of the smallest triangle formed by
any three points.

Keep the code correct, short, deterministic, and easy to understand.
